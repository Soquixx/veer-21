# VEER-21 visual assets

## Included local assets

- `public/assets/veer-roundel.svg` — original VEER-21 roundel artwork created for this interface; it is not an official government or service insignia.
- `public/assets/tricolour-waves.svg` — original saffron/white/green background artwork.
- `public/assets/aircraft-silhouette.svg` — original abstract aircraft silhouette artwork.

These local assets are self-contained and do not require an external image host.

## Searched reference assets

The following Wikimedia Commons assets were reviewed as visual references only. They are not embedded as external dependencies in the app:

- [Roundel of India.svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Roundel_of_India.svg) — Wikimedia Commons describes this geometric Indian Air Force roundel as public domain; its page notes that separate restrictions may apply to official insignia.
- [Sukhoi SU-30MKI.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sukhoi_SU-30MKI.jpg) — photograph by g4sp, licensed CC BY-SA 2.0. If the photograph is ever included, keep attribution to the photographer and license, and do not imply endorsement.
- [Indian Air Force Dassault Mirage 2000.JPG](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Indian_Air_Force_Dassault_Mirage_2000.JPG) — U.S. Air Force photograph marked public domain in the United States on Commons.

The reference screenshot is used for broad visual direction only; the UI does not use the Ministry of Defence / DRDO seal and does not claim to be an official government service.
